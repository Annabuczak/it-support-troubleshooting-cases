## About these write-ups

These case studies are based on my workplace support notes. Recorded actions and findings are distinguished from hypotheses, recommendations and outstanding checks. A restored service does not always mean its underlying cause was confirmed.

Client and employer identifiers, personal names, email addresses, domains, internal addresses and original ticket identifiers have been omitted or generalised. Original screenshots, logs, correspondence, credentials and customer files are not published here.

The [command reference](docs/command-reference.md) contains optional diagnostic examples, separate from the actions recorded in the cases.
