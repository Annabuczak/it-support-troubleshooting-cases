## Reading these cases

These documents are adapted from a supplied troubleshooting summary. They are not independent audits of the underlying tickets or logs. Recorded findings are distinguished from hypotheses, recommendations and outstanding work. No case claims a verified end-to-end fix.

The cases illustrate evidence gathering, fault isolation, interpretation of diagnostic messages, clear support boundaries and accurate outcome reporting. Proposed investigations do not imply that those actions were performed.

## Confidentiality

Personal names, customer and employer identifiers, email addresses, domains, application identifiers and business-specific descriptions have been removed or generalised. Exact message sizes and mailbox limits have also been omitted. Public technology names and generic diagnostic errors are retained where useful.

No original screenshots, logs, correspondence, credentials or customer files are included. Anonymisation does not establish permission to disclose employer-derived work. Publication remains subject to the employer's confidentiality requirements and the author's confirmation that these sanitised accounts may be shared.
